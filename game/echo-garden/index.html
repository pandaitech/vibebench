A tiny field guide

How to play

Make a path, rewind it, then let your past selves help with the next one.

✦

Move the firefly

Swipe on the board, tap the arrows, or use arrow keys and WASD. You have 12 successful steps in each attempt.

↺

Leave an echo

Tap Rewind when a path is useful. Your movement becomes a translucent friend that repeats it every step.

▣

Hold the garden open

Echoes can rest on square plates to open matching gates. Light every lantern, then reach the moon gate.

Tip: a blocked echo simply waits on its tile.
